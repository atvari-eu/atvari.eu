Digital sovereignty means you, not a vendor, decide where your systems run,
who can access your data, and what happens when a provider changes direction.
It isn't a product you buy once; it's a property of how you build and operate
software.

We treat it as an engineering problem, not a slogan: it's
[one of our values](@/values/_index.md), and sovereignty is what you get when
your foundations are open, reproducible, and portable.
