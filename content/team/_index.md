## Felix Schröter — Founder & Principal Engineer

Felix is an independent software and cloud engineer with 11+ years of professional experience, building cloud-native systems following DevOps practices for startups and ventures. Before founding atvari in 2026, he worked as Tech Lead at upsquared GmbH and as a Senior Software Engineer at Digital Spring and upsquared, starting his career as a trainee at Werum IT Solutions in 2012.

He has been a **Nixpkgs contributor since 2019**, a **maintainer since 2020**, and a **committer since 2024** — helping maintain the largest open source package repository.

**Focus areas:** Rust · Nix & NixOS · Cloud-native infrastructure & Kubernetes · Software & cloud architecture · DevOps & Infrastructure as Code

**Education:** M.Sc. Computer Science, University of Colorado Boulder (2024–2026)

**Languages:** German (native), English (professional working proficiency)

**Notable project:** Plastikpost — a web app that has recorded 79,000+ objections to unsolicited advertising mail.
