what purpose, and on what legal basis, in accordance with the EU General Data
Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the German Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG).

### 2. Hosting and server log files

This website is served as static files via **Codeberg Pages**, a service
operated by Codeberg e.V. (Germany).

When you access the website, the hosting infrastructure processes the technical
information your browser transmits, in particular:

- IP address
- date and time of the request
- requested page/file and access status
- browser type and version, operating system
- referrer URL

This data is processed to deliver the website, to keep it stable and secure, and
to detect and defend against attacks. The legal basis is our legitimate interest
in a secure, functioning website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not combine this data
with other sources in order to identify you.

Server log files are retained only for as long as necessary for these purposes,
and are then deleted or anonymised.

### 3. Analytics (Plausible)

We use **Plausible Analytics**, a privacy-friendly analytics service operated by
Plausible Insights OÜ (Estonia, EU), loaded from `plausible.io`.

Plausible works without cookies and without collecting personal data. There is no
cross-site tracking, no user profiling, and no sharing of data with advertising
networks. All data is processed and stored on servers in the European Union. We
receive only aggregated, anonymised statistics (for example, number of visitors,
pages viewed, and referrers).

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in understanding how our website is
used so that we can improve it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### 4. Contacting us

You can reach us by email ([hi@atvari.eu](mailto:hi@atvari.eu)) or phone. When you
contact us, we process the data you provide (typically your name, email address,
and the content of your message) in order to answer your request.

The legal basis is the performance of a contract or the taking of steps at your
request prior to entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and our legitimate
interest in responding to enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep this data until
your request has been fully handled and any statutory retention periods have
expired.

The contact form on this website does not send data to our servers: it opens your
own email application with the message prefilled, so nothing is transmitted until
you send it.

### 5. Scheduling a call

Our contact page links to **Proton Calendar** (Proton AG, Switzerland) so that you
can book an appointment. This is an external link, not an embedded widget: we
transfer data to Proton only when you click through and use the service. From
that point on, Proton's own privacy policy applies.

### 6. External links

Our website contains links to external services (for example GitHub, LinkedIn,
Bluesky, Mastodon, Crunchbase, and OpenStreetMap). These are ordinary hyperlinks:
no data is transmitted to those providers until you click one and open their site.

### 7. Cookies and local storage

We do not set cookies and we do not use tracking or advertising cookies. That is
why no cookie banner is shown: none is required for what we do.

For your theme preference (light/dark) we store a single entry in your browser's
`localStorage`. It stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and is used
only to remember the display mode you chose. It is necessary for the function you
requested.

- receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20 GDPR),
- object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR),
- withdraw any consent you have given at any time (Art. 7(3) GDPR).
