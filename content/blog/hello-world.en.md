**atvari GmbH is alive!** 🥳 We officially opened for business earlier this month, and today we're launching our new website.

atvari is a software and cloud engineering consultancy based in Lüneburg, Germany, working with clients worldwide. We help teams design, build, and operate secure, scalable, and easy-to-maintain systems, offering software & cloud architecture, custom software engineering, cloud engineering, and DevOps consulting, with particular depth in Rust and the Nix ecosystem.

After 11+ years building cloud-native systems for startups and larger businesses, I wanted to offer that experience directly to teams who need it, without the overhead of a big consultancy.

I'm also an active [Nixpkgs](https://github.com/NixOS/nixpkgs) contributor, maintainer, and committer, and I care deeply about digital sovereignty: building on open, auditable foundations instead of black boxes, so the systems we ship are secure and reproducible by design.

This is only the beginning. Follow along here or on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/atvari), [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/atvari.eu), or [Mastodon](https://fosstodon.org/@atvari) for updates. We already have an interesting partnership in the works, more on that soon.

If you need help with Rust, Nix, or software & cloud engineering more generally, [get in touch](@/contact/_index.md), we'd love to hear from you.

— Felix Schröter, Founder
