❄️ **atvari GmbH is a [Bronze sponsor of NixCon 2026](https://2026.nixcon.org/#sponsors)**.

[NixCon](https://2026.nixcon.org/) is the annual gathering of the Nix community, where developers, operators, researchers, and enthusiasts share ideas, present new work, and explore the future of reproducible systems. This year's edition takes place from 25 to 28 September 2026 at the Jagiellonian University Auditorium Maximum in Kraków, Poland, with 600–700 attendees expected across two program days and two hack/unconference days, including the very first NixCon CTF.

Sponsoring NixCon is a natural fit for us. We've built our practice around Nix and NixOS specifically: reproducible builds, deployment, CI, and the harder migrations that come with adopting Nix in an existing enterprise. Our founder has been a [Nixpkgs](https://github.com/NixOS/nixpkgs) contributor since 2019, a maintainer since 2020, and a committer since 2024. Supporting the conference that brings this community together is part of giving back to the ecosystem our business is built on.

Are you going to NixCon? We'd love to meet, [get in touch](@/contact/_index.md) beforehand or just come say hi in Kraków.

👉 [See the NixCon 2026 sponsors](https://2026.nixcon.org/#sponsors)
