Europe's own rules (the GDPR, the Data Act, the Cyber Resilience Act, and the
push for a European digital identity) already assume a degree of control that
many non-European cloud and SaaS stacks quietly undermine: data exported to
other jurisdictions, terms changed unilaterally, and critical services that
can be cut off without recourse.

Owning your stack is how you meet those rules, not fight them.
